Online Privacy

About Online Privacy

Online Privacy is a free guide to staying safe on phones and the internet in India: how the common frauds work and how to recognise them, what to do in the first hour after a fraud, step-by-step privacy and security settings for WhatsApp, Android, iPhone, Google, Instagram and Facebook, and three small tools that run on your own device.

Official facts and where we read them

Helpline numbers, portals and rules on this site were read on the official websites named below on 12 November 2026. Nothing of this kind is written from memory.

  • 1930 cyber fraud helpline and the reporting portal: National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal, cybercrime.gov.in.
  • Chakshu, lost-phone blocking, mobile connections in your name, IMEI check, 1909 and SMS sender names: the Sanchar Saathi portal of the Department of Telecommunications, sancharsaathi.gov.in, and its lost-handset pages at ceir.gov.in.
  • Customer liability for unauthorised electronic banking transactions: Reserve Bank of India circular RBI/2017-18/15 dated 6 July 2017, as published on rbi.org.in.
  • National Consumer Helpline 1915: consumerhelpline.gov.in.
  • Tele MANAS 14416: telemanas.mohfw.gov.in.

Rules, numbers and portals change. Each page that uses them carries the date, and you should confirm on the official site before relying on them.

Everything else is our own writing

The descriptions of how each fraud works, the warning signs and the advice are written by us from widely reported patterns; they are general guidance, not an account of any particular case. The settings walk-throughs use the menu names of WhatsApp, Android 13 to 15 (Pixel-style phones), iOS 17 and 18, Google accounts, Instagram and Facebook as they stood in November 2026. App makers move and rename menus, and phone makers differ, so a name may not match your screen exactly; each guide says so.

The tools store nothing

The message checker, the link checker and the password generator run entirely in your browser. What you paste or generate is not sent to us and not saved. The ticks on the checklists are kept in your own browser so that you can continue later.
